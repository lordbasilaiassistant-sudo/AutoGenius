# Momentum Flux Lemma

For an ideal gas with isotropic velocity distribution, the pressure on a wall is given by P = nm⟨vx²⟩, where n is the number density, m is the molecular mass, and ⟨vx²⟩ is the mean square velocity component perpendicular to the wall.
