# Dimensional Consistency Requirement

Physical laws and Lagrangians must be dimensionally consistent. A Lagrangian must have dimensions of energy (ML²T⁻²), and all terms in the Lagrangian must have the same dimensions. Dimensional inconsistency renders the formalism physically meaningless.
