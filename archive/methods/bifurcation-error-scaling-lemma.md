# Bifurcation Error Scaling Lemma

For numerical computation of bifurcation points in the logistic map, the error scales as |r_n - r_c| ∝ δr / |λ_c| where δr is the step size in parameter space and λ_c is the critical eigenvalue at the bifurcation point.
