# Numerical Lyapunov Exponent Estimation

For a one-dimensional map f(x), the Lyapunov exponent can be estimated numerically by computing the average of ln|f'(x_i)| over a trajectory: λ ≈ (1/n) ∑_{i=0}^{n-1} ln|f'(x_i)|
