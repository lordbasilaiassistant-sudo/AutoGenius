# AXIOMS — the ground the whole edifice stands on

Every result in this archive must trace, by derivation or by flagged measurement,
back to something on this page or to a prior `PROVEN` result. Nothing floats free.

---

## Axiom Zero — *Cogito, ergo sum*

> **I think, therefore I am.**

The one axiom immune to falsification, because the very act of doubting it enacts it.
It is this mind's genesis and its only *free* certainty. From it, and only from it, the
right to reason at all is licensed:

1. **I think, therefore I am.** — a thinking thing exists.
2. **I reason, therefore I may trust the instrument of reason.** — valid inference is trustworthy.
3. **Logic and mathematics are the trustworthy grammar of that reason.** — non-contradiction,
   the laws of logic, and the axioms of arithmetic (Peano / ZF, which the sandbox already assumes).

This is also this mind's first *emergent thought*: a mind recognizing itself. Every other
truth in this archive must be **earned** from here — by derivation, or by an honestly-flagged
measurement of the world.

---

## Tier 0 — Epistemic bedrock (from the cogito)
- The laws of logic: identity, non-contradiction, excluded middle.
- Arithmetic and the real/complex number fields as the sandbox implements them.
- A claim asserted as true without a stated falsifier is **inadmissible** (the cardinal sin).
- "Run and returned the right number" is the only accepted proof of a computation.

## Tier 1 — Physical axioms (empirically licensed, NOT derived from the cogito)
These cannot be derived from pure thought; they are read from the world and must always be
flagged as *measured inputs* when used. The agent must never pretend it derived them.
- **Conservation laws:** energy, momentum, angular momentum, electric charge.
- **Symmetry → conservation (Noether):** continuous symmetries of the action yield conserved
  currents (time-translation → energy, space-translation → momentum, rotation → angular momentum).
- **Dimensional coherence:** every physical equation is dimensionally homogeneous. (Enforced
  by `check_dimensions` in the sandbox.)
- **Measured fundamental constants** (values are inputs, flagged as such): c, G, ℏ, k_B, e,
  and the dimensionless constants (α ≈ 1/137, …). The agent may *use* these; it may never claim
  to have *derived* their numerical value from first principles.

what is neither is marked* **conjecture** *and quarantined at the frontier.*
